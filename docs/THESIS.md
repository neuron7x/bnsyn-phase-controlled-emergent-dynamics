# BN-Syn Thesis

BN-Syn studies how capabilities emerge in neural systems before they become opaque.

The project tests the hypothesis that capability emergence is preceded by measurable causal invariants.

Core research question:
Do neural systems exhibit universal order parameters that predict capability onset?

BN-Syn provides a controlled fail-closed laboratory where emergence can be induced, measured, and verified.

The long-term goal is to identify cross-architecture emergence invariants that may transfer across neural systems, including transformer-based models.
